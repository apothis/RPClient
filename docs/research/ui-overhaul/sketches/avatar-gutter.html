Trade-offs. lg 24pt: avatar is a small leading marker; transcript reads as primarily about the prose. Less character-presence, more transcript-feel — closer to Open WebUI's posture, but Open WebUI doesn't have characters. xl 32pt: avatar carries real visual weight; the character feels "present" at every turn. Closer to a Discord-style avatar prominence but inside a no-bubbles transcript layout. RPClient's RP frame benefits from the character feeling present (per the user's "chat-feel, not form-feel" north-star) — but 32pt avatars start to dominate short turns and may feel busy in a long transcript. Open question: does the user want presence (B) or restraint (A)?
